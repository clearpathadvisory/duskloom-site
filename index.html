Questions

Reasonable things to ask

Does it really not loop?

Really. There are no audio files in the app to loop. Noise is generated sample by sample, and events like raindrops, thunder and crackles are scheduled at random times as they occur. Leave the same mix running for eight hours and no two minutes are identical.

Will it keep playing when my phone locks?

Yes. It declares itself an audio app to iOS and runs a playback service on Android, so it plays all night with the screen off and appears on your lock screen.

Do you collect anything?

No, and not as a policy choice — the app makes no network requests, so there is no channel through which anything could leave your phone. Put it in airplane mode and it behaves identically. Levels and saved mixes stay on the device.

What is free, exactly?

All seven noise colours plus rain, ocean, wind, fireplace, box fan, stream and crickets. Unlimited playback, background audio, the sleep timer and the breathing guide are never locked. Tap any paid sound and it plays for thirty seconds, so you hear what you would be buying before you pay.

Is there a subscription?

No, and there will not be one. A subscription pays for recording, licensing and hosting. We have none of those costs.

Does it help with tinnitus?

Many people use coloured noise for it, and Duskloom generates white, pink, brown, green, blue, grey and violet noise properly rather than playing a recording of them. We make no medical claim — speak to an audiologist about what is right for you.
